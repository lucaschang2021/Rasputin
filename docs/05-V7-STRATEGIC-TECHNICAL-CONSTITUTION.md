> **Status:** PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE FREEZE

## 1. Constitutional Definition

Rasputin is the sovereign computational capital control plane that decides where intelligence, compute, trust, attention and risk are worth spending; governs the resulting execution; adversarially tests its own decisions; survives failure; and continuously reallocates resources toward risk-adjusted outcome value.

Rasputin does not compete with frontier models on intelligence. It governs heterogeneous intelligence as scarce capital.

## 2. Permanent First Principles

1. **Intelligence is capital.** Money, compute, quota, time, verification, human attention, risk, irreversibility and recovery capacity are scarce.
2. **Execution is the atomic economic object.** Portfolio-level decisions must still resolve to reconstructable executions.
3. **Portfolio before routing.** Decide whether and how much to fund before choosing a model.
4. **Outcome before output.** Optimize downstream value, not cosmetic response quality.
5. **Authority before autonomy.** Agents never create their own authority.
6. **Adversarial assurance is continuous.** The system attacks its own allocator, policy, runtime and evidence assumptions.
7. **Recovery is an investment decision.** Recovery is bounded by expected value, capital, risk and authority.
8. **Frontier theory belongs in Core; frontier technology is an Adapter by default.**

## 5. Capital Thesis

A workload is an investment opportunity for scarce computational resources.

The allocator may choose:


Nominal API price is not the true economic price. Rasputin considers scarcity, opportunity cost, verification burden, risk and recovery reserve.

## 9. Red-Blue Constitution

Red-Blue Assurance is a permanent cross-layer immune system.

The Red side must be allowed to challenge:

- allocator value/scarcity assumptions;
- policy boundaries;
- tool/MCP/A2A authority;
- memory/context integrity;
- verifier reliability;
- telemetry/outcome integrity;
- provider/quota/latency assumptions;
- recovery logic.

The Blue side may deny, contain, quarantine, reduce privilege, tighten policy, escalate verification, rollback, substitute resources or terminate.

The protected property is **Computational Capital Integrity**.

## 10. Adaptive Recovery Constitution

Permanent recovery loop:

```text
DETECT -> DIAGNOSE -> CONTAIN -> RECOVER -> VERIFY -> REALLOCATE -> LEARN
```

Recovery is not equivalent to retry. It may change resource, harness, tool, memory, runtime, verification level or portfolio allocation, but never widens authority implicitly.

Recovery must stop when authorized recovery capital or risk capacity is exhausted, or when expected remaining value no longer justifies expected recovery cost.

## 13. Trust Constitution

Evidence progresses from local integrity to stronger trust:

```text
structured record
 -> content commitment
 -> hash chain
 -> Merkle batch
 -> signature / transparency
 -> runtime / hardware attestation
 -> optional external anchor
 -> future Zero-Knowledge Compliance
```

Rasputin does not claim to prove hidden chain-of-thought.

## 14. Benchmark Constitution

No major optimization, recovery or safety claim without reproducible benchmark evidence.

### RCB

Measures portfolio utility, effective cost, verified success, SLA, capital/risk use and ROCC.

### RARB

Measures attack detection/bypass, unauthorized effects, MTTD, MTTR, recovery success/cost, blast radius and utility under attack/failure.

## 16. Constitutional Scope Firewall

A capability enters Core only if it improves at least one of:

1. risk-adjusted outcome value;
2. capital allocation efficiency;
3. authority / safety;
4. resilience / recovery efficiency;
5. verifiability;
6. interoperability;
7. learning quality.

Otherwise it remains Adapter, Commodity, Research or Deferred.
